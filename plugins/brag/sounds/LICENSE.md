# Sound effects

The `.ogg` files in this folder are from [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/) (the Casino, Impact Sounds, Interface Sounds, UI Audio and keyboard packs), released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): free to use, change and redistribute, with no attribution required. They came by way of [latent-spaces/brag](https://github.com/latent-spaces/brag), whose per-file analysis (duration, brightness, high-frequency risk, suggested uses) is summarised in `index.json`; brag is MIT-licensed (Copyright (c) 2026 Shunit Haviv Hakimi).
